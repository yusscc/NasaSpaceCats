July 20th, 1976. Viking 1 is the first American space probe to safely land on Mars.
It takes the first color images from the Martian surface and digs in the dirt there.
It analyses the Martian soil for signs of life. The findings are inconclusive, and most experts chalk it up to chemical reactions.
Originally intended to last for 90 days, it kept operating for over six years. The connection was lost in 1982.
