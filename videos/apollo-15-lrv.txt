July 30, 1971. Apollo 15 makes a moon landing near Apennine mountains, bringing a small collapsible car.
With the help of an electrically powered rover, David Scott and James Irwin travel almost 28 kilometers.
Near the mountains they find a so-called "Genesis rock," a sample of the Moon that is 4 billion years old.
Before departing, they aim the rover's camera at the module to show Earth the take-off.
