July 30th, 1971. Apollo 15 lands by the Apennine mountains, with a folded-up car.
On this battery-powered rover, David Scott and James Irwin drive about 28 kilometres.
Near the mountains they find the Genesis Rock, a piece of the Moon about 4 billion years old.
Before leaving, they aim the rover's camera at the lander, so Earth can watch them lift off.
