April 20, 1967. A robotic lander named Surveyor 3 makes its gentle landing in the Ocean of Storms.
Using a small shovel, it digs the first trench ever dug in the surface of the Moon, proving its solidity for future humans.
The crew of Apollo 12 lands close to it in November 1969 and heads to see it.
The crew takes the camera back to Earth, leaving other remnants on the Moon intact, the only man-made robot visited on another world by man.
