April 20th, 1967. A robot called Surveyor 3 lands softly in the Ocean of Storms.
With a little scoop, it digs the Moon's first trench, and shows the ground can hold astronauts.
In November 1969, the Apollo 12 crew lands close by and walks over to visit it.
They take its camera home. The rest is still there: the only robot people have visited on another world.
