Feb 18, 2021. Small helicopter named Ingenuity lands on Mars along with the Perseverance rover.
April 19, 2021 - first ever powered flight on any other planet; atmosphere is just 1% that of Earth’s.
Designed to fly in 5 tests in total, it manages 72 flights, preparing the way for the rover.
Jan 2024 - damage to the blades forces it into ground, however it remains on the red planet to this day.
