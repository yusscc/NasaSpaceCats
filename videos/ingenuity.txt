February 18th, 2021. A tiny helicopter, Ingenuity, lands on Mars under the Perseverance rover.
On April 19th, it makes the first powered flight on another planet, in air about 1% as thick as Earth's.
Planned for five test flights, it flies 72 times, scouting the way for Perseverance.
In January 2024, a damaged rotor grounds it. It still stands on Mars today.
