January 25th, 2004. The rover Opportunity lands on the plains of Meridiani, on Mars.
It finds tiny round stones nicknamed blueberries, and rocks that could only form in water.
Planned for 90 Martian days, it drives about 45 kilometres and lasts almost 15 years.
In 2018, a planet-wide dust storm hides the Sun. Opportunity goes silent for good.
